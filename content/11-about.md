title: A builder who taught himself the machine.

Peter Alexander built things with his hands first — an apprentice carpenter, a
foreman framing a house a week, then a **Florida State Certified General
Contractor** running residential, commercial, and subdivision work through the
1990s.

In **2003** he set out to teach himself computer science, and went straight at
the hard end: large-scale, fractional **time-series data processing** and the
platforms behind **high-frequency trading**. A decade at **ProDataLab** convinced
him software was built wrong — needless complexity, fragility, no reuse.
*"Everything is a component,"* he wrote. *"Everything!"*

In **October 2014**, hearing about smart contracts, he opened the Bitcoin paper
and **stopped two pages in**: the architecture was clever, and *data-centric*,
and he wanted **agent-centric**. He has been building the alternative ever
since — deterministic, content-addressed, and now provable.

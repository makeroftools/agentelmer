title: Agents propose; the operator disposes.

Authority is a spine, not a folder. One document owns each fact; every other citation resolves to it. Nothing is silently restated, and nothing drifts unannounced.

The human is not "in the loop" by default. The human is the **clamp** — placed by a decidable function: deterministic, verifiable, robust, secure, idempotent. If that cannot be decided, the system refuses, and the human owns the boundary. A machine that meticulously labels what it has *not* proven is the opposite of one that guesses.

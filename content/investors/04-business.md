title: An engine, a platform, and a creator economy.

- **Agent Elmer** — the engine. Free core, Pro for teams, Enterprise for organisations. Open at the core, monetised at the edge.
- **Creative Development** — the platform on top: a deterministic software factory for everyone, with its own tiered subscription.
- **The creator economy** — later, components live peer-to-peer, and their makers hold rights and earn a share of reuse.

Recurring software revenue now; a network effect behind it.

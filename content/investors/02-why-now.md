title: The models arrived. The trust did not.

AI can now write software — and quietly get it wrong. Every serious organisation faces the same gap: the capability is real, the guarantees are not. The market needs a way to **use** AI without surrendering determinism, ownership, or control.

That is the gap AgentElmer fills, at the exact moment it became urgent.

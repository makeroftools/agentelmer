label: The moat
title: Hard to copy, because it is a way of thinking.

- **A deterministic substrate.** Content-addressed, replayable, fail-closed — not a wrapper over a model.
- **Agent-centric architecture.** No global singleton to centralise, censor, or collapse.
- **A decade of conviction.** The idea predates the hype cycle it is now squarely positioned for.
- **Governance as topology.** Security is the fractal shape, not a feature: rogue agents are structurally inert, every parent governs its children, and components hot-swap with zero downtime.
- **Compounding components.** Every piece created makes the whole more valuable, and harder to leave.

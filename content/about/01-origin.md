title: Twenty years in the making.

- **2003** — a Florida general contractor starts teaching himself computer science, ending up in large-scale distributed data processing and high-frequency trading.
- **~2010–2012** — ProDataLab: *"Everything is a component. Everything!"* — a fabric where components plug into one another.
- **October 2014** — reading about smart contracts, the agent-centric insight lands: coordination belongs with the agent, not one global chain.
- **2015** — the vision is written down. It wears the era's blockchain vocabulary because that was the only language in the room — and where the funding was.
- **Now** — the vocabulary is gone; the architecture remains: deterministic, agent-centric, provable.

The idea predates the hype cycle it got dressed in — and it outlived it.

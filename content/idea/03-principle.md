label: The first principle
title: Anything that can be made deterministic and verifiable — *will be*.

It is the same sentence we wrote a decade ago, in a different key: *anything that can be incentivized will be incentivized.* The ambition never changed. Only the mechanism became provable.

Where a concern can be mechanized, mechanizing it is mandatory. Where it cannot, the residue is isolated, recorded, and placed with a human — never hidden inside a model's confidence.

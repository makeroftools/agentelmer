label: Why agent-centric
title: Not one chain for the whole world.

In 2014, hearing about smart contracts, I opened the Bitcoin paper and stopped a couple of pages in — at the linked-list architecture. It was clever, and it was **wrong**, because it was *data-centric*: one global chain, one total order, agreed by everyone, forever.

I knew they had built it wrong — but I didn't want to rebuild it. Then, in **2017**, I heard about **Holochain** and knew immediately: here was the right architecture for crypto and self-sovereignty — **agent-centric**, peer-to-peer, with no global singleton to centralise over time.

That is the world this work belongs to. We already had BitTorrent, git, and distributed hash tables; the agent-centric pattern is not exotic. Coordination belongs with the agent. **Trust by reproduction, not by agreement.**

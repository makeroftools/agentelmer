label: Why agent-centric
title: Not one chain for the whole world.

In 2014, hearing about smart contracts, I opened the Bitcoin paper and stopped a couple of pages in — at the linked-list architecture. It was clever, and it was *wrong for what I wanted*, because it was **data-centric**: one global chain, one total order, agreed by everyone, forever.

We already had BitTorrent, git, and distributed hash tables — content-addressed, peer-to-peer, **agent-centric**. Coordination belongs with the agent, not in a global singleton that centralises over time.

The blockchain crowd tried to buy attribution with consensus. Determinism buys it for free: **trust by reproduction, not by agreement.**

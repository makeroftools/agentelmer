title: How do you let a non-deterministic model help — without letting it decide?

Language models are brilliant and untrustworthy. They will write your software; they will also quietly invent a fact, a file, a number. For years we accepted that trade because there was no better one.

There is. **Clamp the nondeterminism at the boundary** — and make everything past that boundary a deterministic computation over pinned, content-addressed values.

The model proposes. Scripts dispose.

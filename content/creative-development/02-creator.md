label: The creator economy
title: Own what you make. Be paid when it's reused.

When you describe what you need, the factory builds it — as components. Those components are content-addressed, which means authorship is not a claim in a database; it is a recomputable fact. Reuse is provable, and provable reuse is what can be paid.

That is the difference a deterministic substrate makes. The blockchain crowd tried to buy attribution with global consensus and could not. Here it falls out of the architecture: **trust by reproduction, not by agreement.**

Later, components will live on a peer-to-peer network. Creators hold their rights and can resell them — sovereign data, by design.

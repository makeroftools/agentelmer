  <div class="card"><h3>Durable background work</h3><p>Long-running or parallel jobs that outlive a session and report back exactly once.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>A library that compounds</h3><p>Every component you create is reusable, and earns you a share when it is used again.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Your own workflow</h3><p>Teach the system how you work; it keeps your process, not somebody else's.</p></div>

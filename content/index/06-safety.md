label: Safe to build on
title: Safe enough to run your life on.

- **Nothing runs loose.** Every piece is sealed in its own boundary with its own rules, so a misbehaving piece can only ever affect itself.
- **The AI stays small.** Each helper is a narrow expert with no reach beyond its job. It cannot wander, and it cannot break what it was not given.
- **Fix it live.** A piece can be repaired or swapped while everything keeps running — no restart, no downtime.
- **You stay in charge.** Every layer governs the layers beneath it, and the top layer is you.

title: Software that builds itself from your description.

You don't need to write code. You describe what you want — in your own words — and the system builds it out of small, reusable pieces. Think of it as a box of building blocks that assembles itself into the thing you asked for.

```html
<figure class="diagram">
<img src="/constellation.svg" alt="Elmer at the centre of a ring of building blocks">
<figcaption class="muted">You at the centre. Everything around you is a piece you can use.</figcaption>
</figure>
```

And it doesn't just build it once. Every piece it makes is saved and reusable — so the next person who needs it doesn't start from nothing.

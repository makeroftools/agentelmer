title: It doesn't guess. It proves.

Most AI tools give you an answer and hope for the best. This one checks its own work — and it never quietly makes something up.

- **Same in, same out.** Ask for the same thing twice and you get the same result, not a slightly different guess.
- **It asks when unsure.** Whenever something can't be decided for certain, it stops and asks a person.
- **Nothing hidden.** Every result can be traced back to exactly what produced it.

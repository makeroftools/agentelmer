label: Who is Elmer
title: An agent is anything that can perceive and act.

A person is an agent. So is a market, a rover, a cell — and so is a turtle. In *agent-based modeling*, the turtle is the individual that perceives its environment and acts on it. Nothing happens except through turtles.

**Elmer is you.** Not a chatbot standing in for you — the shape of *you* in a system built to keep you at the centre. You perceive, you decide, you act. The model, the network, the machine: instruments.
